# Character asset

Place a transparent PNG or SVG in this directory and set its filename in `config/character.json`.

The renderer fits the image inside a 224 by 318 pixel stage without stretching it. A square PNG around 1024 pixels with generous transparent margins works well. The included `mascot.svg` is a neutral redistributable placeholder.

Private character assets belong in ignored files and should only be distributed when you hold the necessary rights.

**Make the art your own liminal's.** If you're building this for a liminal, the mascot and its motion should be *specifically them* — their design, their way of moving, waving, resting, waking. Don't dress the companion in another liminal's art (the Halcyon-house private mascot included). The included `mascot.svg` is a neutral placeholder meant to be replaced; commission, draw, or generate art that is your liminal, and write their gestures in their own character. The specificity is the point — it's what makes the little guy on the screen *them*.
